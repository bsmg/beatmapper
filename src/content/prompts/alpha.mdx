title: Welcome to Beatmapper!
---

It's probably been a while since you last checked out Beatmapper. Or maybe it's your first time dropping by!
In any case, here's what you need to know about the current state of the editor:

- Beatmapper is *alpha* software. That means things can spontaneously combust at any moment. Make sure to keep frequent backups of your maps using <Shortcut>meta+p</Shortcut>.
- We have really thorough docs! Please [check them out](https://beatmapper.app/docs), since they will probably answer a good majority of the questions you might have.
- Beatmapper is open-source! Contributions are welcome if you know what your doing, so check out [the guide](https://github.com/bsmg/beatmapper/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) if you're interested.
