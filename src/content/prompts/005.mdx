title: "New Update: Grids & Gradients"
---

A new feature drop has finally arrived! Here are some of the highlights of what you can expect to find:

- A [standalone map converter](/convert)
- Basic timescale support for vanilla BPM changes
- New [placement modes](/docs/manual/notes#obstacle-placement-modes) for obstacles
- Full support for [color boost](/docs/manual/events#color-boost-tracks) and basic transition events
- Improved [filtering](/docs/manual/events#track-visibility) and [mirroring](/docs/manual/events#mirror-lock) workflows for basic tracks
- Full support for [vanilla color scheme overrides](/docs/manual/publishing#editing-color-schemes)
- Tons of quality-of-life improvements and bugfixes

This update is pretty jam-packed with new features, so check out the [release notes](/docs/releases/0.5) if you want a full breakdown on everything that's new and improved.
